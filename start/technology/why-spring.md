## Why AmadeusWeb Spring Matters

AmadeusWeb Spring is a way to build a lasting digital home for a person, a mission, a team or a wider network. It keeps content in ordinary files, backs those files up to Git and lets people develop and review work offline before it is published.

Two documents explain the approach in different ways:

- [How It Works](%url%in-2026/how-it-works/) describes the philosophy, workflow and practical foundations of the platform.
- [The Deep Dive](../deep-dive/) explains the structure, terminology, technical choices and broader vision in more detail.

Together, they help a visitor understand why a website can be more than a collection of promotional pages. It can be a carefully planned, transparent and evolving record of what a person or group is learning, offering and building.

---

## Useful to the General Public

The two documents are useful to anyone trying to understand what a simpler web can do for them. They explain:

- How a website can begin with a **three-year outline** of the content, activities and audiences it needs to support.
- Why files, folders and Git can make content easier to review, recover and carry forward.
- How offline-first development lets a lead review work before it is checked in and deployed.
- How sections, nodes, sub nodes and pages create navigation that mirrors the organisation behind the site.
- Why responsive HTML is preferred for new content, with PDFs retained for legacy material and document repositories.
- How Markdown, HTML, PHP, sheets, decks, forms, media and AI conversations can share one ecosystem.
- Why public repositories and published WithAI conversations make the development process more transparent.
- How the `%cdn%` variable keeps shared assets from bloating individual sites or federated sections.
- Why direct, structured email drafts can support engagement without system email, logins or captchas.
- How the platform supports an internet of missions rather than another algorithm-controlled attention system.

The documents are also a starting point for a conversation: What is your mission, what audiences do you serve and what skills would help you build a durable digital presence?

## A Shared Starting Point

The audiences are different, but the foundation is shared: **clear purpose, organised content, visible history, local review and gradual publishing**. AmadeusWeb Spring gives each audience room to develop its own voice while connecting it to a wider Dynamic AmadeusWeb Network.

The next step is to identify the people, activities and knowledge your site must support, then begin arranging the files and folders that will make that mission visible.
